2009 November 21

Saturday, November 21, 2009

3:02 PM

1.  Officer's Meeting, Port Orford

Convened 3:04 PM





2.  Marilyn'sMotion from Dennis Smith ORDTA to add Internet downloads to list of approved music distribution methods in paragraph 1a in VII-6 of P&Ps. Currently reads CD or 45 RPM record. Seems reasonable.
3.  Tony Haskins has letter from River City Horses. Wants to present it tomorrow. Needs a motion first.
4.  Mention dues $3 vs $25 which includes Where/When. Should/is the Where/When fee be mandatory? Where did the one dance per year requirement come from? Something for the CPA to address. Should we change the forms so that the area dues are not included? Maybe Where/When fee should be dropped altogether. In the short term, just eliminate the area dues and add a 0 dances per year option.
5.  Barbi suggests having personal discussion with area delegates and suggest they have discussion with their club delegates.
6.  Was there a motion once to eliminate delegate/officer meetings at Festival meeting?
7.  Delegates mis-heard the insurance instructions. Cover letter seemed to imply that updates could be done online, but they can't really do re-enrollment online. That must still be done with the paper roster forms. Need to clarify this. The delegates need to understand what their responsibility is.
8.  Dianne Barrick lawsuit is going to trial in December in Albany. Kay needs to be prepared to testify. What about expenses? Are there legal requirements about that? Floyd suggests negotiating with USDA to cover her. Who in the Federation has been served? EE people haven't heard anything about being served. Would they necessarily hear if an insurance company is involved?
9.  Floyd has web suggestions. Directory orders could be done online, centralized. Send reminders to people who haven't ordered.
10. Membership info timing. Information due May 1, but there are 100 corrections before information really due at end of July. Perhaps we should change to distributing the forms in May and returning them at Summer Festival. That would eliminate confusion about that 4-month delay. Present that option at the state meeting tomorrow.
11. We used to have a leadership seminar separate from Mid-Winter, as a whole weekend. Floyd would like to start that up again to groom the movers and shakers. Pattern after Washington's success? Recommend doing one in 2011. Keys are location, keynote speakers, content. Zola suggests making the first one free. (Perceived value issue.) Kay suggests scheduling it for a weekend that already has a purpose, like September State meeting. That kind of defeats the purpose. There are clearly a lot of opinions here.
12. Floyd also recommends having a meeting at Mid-Winter for delegates, council presidents, and key Federation officials. Bring people up to date.
13. Zola points out Holiday Inn Express available 12:30 PM to 2:30 PM if we want delegate discussion meeting.



Adjourned 4:07 PM
