## Online Voting Report

## 12 September 2021

## 

I checked on a dozen different online voting services for companies, groups, and non-profits. Somewhat to my surprise, they were all much more expensive than we can justify. Our needs are very simple. It is simple to set up an online voting form using Google Forms; I did that as an example for the officers, and no one had problems. Thus, I would like to have a motion allowing us to run our 2022-23 elections online. Assuming it works with no glitches, we would modify the P&Ps to make that permanent. Each club would still be allowed one vote; the person entering the info will need to enter a name and phone number in case we need to verify that the vote was legitimate.
