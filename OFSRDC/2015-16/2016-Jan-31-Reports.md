OFN Editor's Report



Things continue to run pretty smoothly. As I noted in my column this month, the total number of flyers is down this month. I find that disturbing, but it remains to be seen whether this trend continues. We had a large number of obituaries this month; let's hope that comes to a stop.



If you have people who like to take photos, I do have some tips. Some people like to add captions or decorations to their photos, or make collages out of several photos. I tend not to use those, because the decorations and text are too small in the thumbnail view.



I have written a new job description for the OFN Editor that describes pretty much how I do things. We will be voting on that later. For now, the job still requires someone with computer expertise, but I'm planning to get the last few manual steps automated.
