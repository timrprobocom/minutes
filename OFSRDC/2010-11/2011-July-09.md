Angie presents. Act in 2006 required everyone to file something every year, and required revocation after 3 years of non-filing.



275,000 organization nationwide lost tax-exempt status in June. Now have until end of 2012 to refile Form 1024 but fee is only $100 instead of $400. If you were revoked, you now have to file Form 1120 instead of 990-N.



6 clubs: Braids & Braves, Lebanon Square Circlers, COC, PAC, couple more. Several said they filed 990-N in 2010 but because they didn't file in 2007-08-09 they were revoked. That doesn't seem fair.



Why should they file for tax-exempt status? What's the point? You have income, even though it is all balanced by expenses. Probably no tax due, but form must be filed. What about Oregon tax obligation?



You can be non-profit without being tax-exempt, but you file Form 1120.



Federation could provide help in paying $100 reinstatement fee for Form 1024.



We can distribute the forms to the delegates.



501(c)(7) social clubs are supposed to be for social activities for members. Non-member income has to be tracked. If non-member > 85%, then not qualified. Make students associate members.



She just got suckered into saying that "donations" are not income. I thought we had finally eliminated that.



Taxpayer advocate office might help Scott. There is a local number in Portland.



Mike filed full Form 990 for 2007-08-09 (for Aloha Grange) threatening $35,000 penalty. She thinks he can get the fee abated. Now he's filling 990-N. Why would he have filed the 990?



As an auditor, the main things she looks at in a (c)(7) is non-member income and 1099s. They do try to be more lenient for very small organizations. Callers and cuers should be 1099ed.



When you go out of business, you're supposed to check a box on your final return. If you have administrative dissolution, you have 5 years to reinstate.



IRS has a place where I can look up the 990N filing status.
