**[Unfinished Business]{.underline}**

1.  Tax-exempt committee. Rushed to meet IRS Oct 15 deadline. Many clubs thought EIN was enough. Not so. EIN tossed after 6 years. Several hours on the phone with the IRS established a process for old dropped clubs to get new EIN with grandfathered TE status. If you don't hear from them by Dec 15, call again. If you have $25k in income or $50k in assets, you must file real Form 990. If a club doesn't use this Oct 15 exception, it costs $400 and a Form 1024. Floyd suggests we send paper letter to clubs with deadlines and processes. Bill proposes to contact each club and council individually after Dec 15. We don't know who went through this. Floyd suggests we ask their status. Good idea. By Mid-Winter, we can see how big the problem is and decide whether really need all clubs to be tax-exempt. Need 100% report. That means SASE. Dennis M asks for better information to take to council.
