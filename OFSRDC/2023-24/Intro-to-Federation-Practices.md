2.  Three guiding documents:

    A.  Constitution

    B.  By-laws

    C.  Practices & Procedures

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```
1.  Constitution

    A.  Legal document; filed with State Corporation Division

    B.  Short: only 2 pages

    C.  Describes the purposes and fundamental guiding principles

    D.  Must have non-discrimination clause

    E.  Can only be amended through formal procedure

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```
1.  By-laws

    A.  Also often filed with State Corporation Division

    B.  Only 3 pages

    C.  Describes the duties of the elected officers

```{=html}
<!-- -->
```
1.  Practices & Procedures

    A.  Stuff that would be in the by-laws if it were more convenient

    B.  Describes how we USUALLY do things, but not necessarily how we MUST do things

    C.  Parliamentarian is the owner

    D.  Describes in detail the duties and responsibilities of each elected office, appointed office, and committee chair

    E.  Includes historical information -- past festivals, past benefit dances

    F.  Includes forms and sample letters

    G.  Living document: amended as needed by parliamentarian

    H.  All motions that affect P&P's should identify section and page

    I.  Every elected officer, appointed officer and delegate gets one, and it is passed down with the position

    J.  Every council gets one copy when it is reprinted

    K.  Expensive to reprint fully, so only updates
