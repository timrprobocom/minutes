There are fundamental questions to be answered



-   What is our goal?
    -   Are we just bringing information up to date?
    -   Do we want it to look significantly different?
    -   Are we hoping to do something artistic?


-   What should it DO?
    -   Library of documents
    -   Archive of records
    -   Flyer/brochure to the public
    -   Club and council information
    -   Officer contacts
    -   Links to other associations
    -   Maps
    -   Event calendar? Or is that the OFN?
    -   Discussion boards?


-   What should it look like?
    -   Current design is very 20^th^ Centruy; functional but not splashy
    -   There are lots of models to use
    -   Columns and boxes (using Bootstrap) like Summer Festival sites
    -   Blog or Wordpress
    -   Wiki format, where others can add



Points to consider

-   Would we want to pay for an "artist"? Not sure there is a cost/benefit
-   Would we want to pay someone to redo it?
    -   Tim R is capable of writing and maintaining, but he's no artist
-   Monthly updates should be in the OFN
-   Most dancers are not tech savvy
-   Out committee should come up with guidelines/framework, not micro-specify
-   Is Facebook more useful than the web site?
-   Formatting stuff is easy. Creating new content is HARD.
