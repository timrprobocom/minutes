# Forming and Maintaining a Club

Modified: 2019-07-19 15:47:16 -0700

---

Why?

-   New clubs do form, but not as often as the past
-   Thus, people don't know

Forming a Club

USDA pubs on incorporating and non-profit status

Joining the Federation

-   Have 16 members (some councils require more)
    -   Remember you need to pay for caller, cuer, hall
-   Get an IRS employer ID number
    -   Online, free, 5 minutes
-   Write constitution and by-laws
    -   Samples are available
-   Become a mutual benefit non-profit Oregon corporation
    -   Mutual benefit means for a group of members, not the public
    -   Forms available online, filinginoregon.com
    -   Costs $50
-   Join a council
    -   Usually the closest is the smartest
    -   Some councils have more strict requirements
-   Send paperwork to Membership Chairman
-   Pay dues ($20)
-   Be prepared to pay for insurance

Oregon Federation Considerations

-   You must remain incorporated
    -   Protection from liability
-   You must remain insured
    -   Everyone on the roster must pay
    -   Even associate and honorary members

Oregon State Considerations

-   Remember your club IS a corporation
    -   You must have a president and a secretary
    -   You must have one meeting a year with minutes
-   Renew annually with Oregon Corporation Division
    -   They just want to know you're alive
    -   No fee
    -   Renewal form is sent to the "Registered Agent"
    -   Renewals each month listed in the OFN
-   Easier if you choose one fixed person to be the Registered Agent
    -   Not their fault if you miss renewal because of bad address
    -   Having a long-term agent reduces the likelihood of mail getting lost
    -   Can have president and secretary change with elections
-   If you don't renew, they will dissolve you
-   Fixing that requires $50

IRS Considerations

-   Almost all clubs qualify as tax-exempt 501(c)(7) social clubs
-   One option is to file for formal recognition with Form 1023 and $400
    -   This is silly for most clubs
-   Any group with less than $25k income can "self-declare" as 501(c)(7)
-   Must file the online Form 990-N every year
    -   But only groups already "in the club" can do this
    -   You need to contact TE/GE Support
        -   877-829-5500
        -   Or you can write them
    -   Explain your situation, they'll add you to the list
    -   Eventually
-   Filing 990-N needs to be a treasurer duty every year
    -   Takes 5 minutes
    -   If you miss 3 years in a row, they will revoke tax-exempt status
    -   Only way back is to start over
-   When you change your president, you need to file Form 8822-B
    -   No fee
-   There are limitations on the use of revenue
    -   Arcane phrase "no part of the profit can inure to the benefit of a member"
    -   This means you can't just write a check to a member to help them
    -   Reimbursing documented expenses is fine
    -   Paying for services is fine if you can prove the rate is not unusual
-   Form 1099-MISC
    -   Must be filed in January to anyone you paid more than $600
