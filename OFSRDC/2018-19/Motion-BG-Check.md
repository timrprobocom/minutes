I move that the Background Check section of the Practices & Procedures be modified by deleting the word "youth" from page VII-29 line 3 and page VII-30 line 6. This clarifies that the hired talent at ALL Federation-sponsored dances must be checked, as was intended.



I move that the Background Check section of the Practices & Procedures be modified by adding "and their spouse, partner, or significant other" to line 4 and line 5 of pave VII-30. This adds a requirement that the partners of the Youth Activities Coordinator and the Festival Youth Activities Coordinators be checked, since they tend to be heavily involved in the activities.
