**OFN Futures Report**

**September 20**



I don't see how we can continue as it is. Joyce seems to have stabilized somewhat, but I'm still not sure she's tracking advertising dollars. IF we could find a square dancer to act as business manager to handle the business side, it's possible we could continue with the printed OFN. I'm not sure people want to do that.



After looking over a number of square dance federation web sites, there are a couple of ways we could set things up. One alternative is to make a web site that looks like the printed OFN, with ads of various sizes and club reports. That would be a lot of work. Another alternative is to slim things down, containing a where/when calendar and a list of links to PDFs. Vivian believes very few people read the club reports, and it seems to me that clubs are now focusing on printed PDFs. Thus, perhaps it doesn't make sense to worry about anything smaller than a full-page flyer. That could be automated, so clubs could enter information themselves.



No income, though, unless we sell ads to vendors. We could eliminate when/where and raise the silly $3/year dues.
